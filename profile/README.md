### Interfase — Powering trusted digital ecosystems

**Engineering, with people at its heart.** Since 1974, from Montevideo, Uruguay.

Secure identity, digital signing, credentials and service orchestration for governments and mission-critical organizations.

- **Orchestration framework**: composable building blocks to connect trusted digital services.
- **Adaptive identity**: onboarding, authentication and lifecycle management for people and organizations.
- **Digital credentials**: issuance and verification of trusted credentials.
- **Signing & processes**: signing workflows, batch signing and automation for digital transactions.

We built Uruguay's first telex exchange, its first packet data network and its first internet node, and every digital trust service provider in the country. Today we are more than 170 people, recognized with Uruguay's National Engineering Award.
